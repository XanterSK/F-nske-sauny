Our story

Timo Nakari is a Finnish sauna designer. Since 2005 he has designed and built saunas with co-owner and master joiner Emil Pákozdi, alongside architects and interior designers, in Hungary and across Europe. Our wood comes directly from Thermory in Estonia, where we check its quality in person several times a year.
